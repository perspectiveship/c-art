About me

I have always liked unique and beautiful things. Paintings fit both.

I'm honoured that so many of you like my paintings, too, as I'm often too critical of myself.

My name is Marta Ciesielska. I hate bragging or talking about myself, so I promise to keep it short. For my whole life, I have been chasing creative journeys, and my recent love is painting. I'm not the best in the world, but I'm good enough.

I'm selling my paintings because I love to paint and want to share these beautiful and unique things. Painting something someone dreams of, so they can hang it on their wall, is one of the best things I've done in my life. Also, my flat is running out of wall space, so I'm glad you like the paintings I've already made!
