How a commission works

01

The conversation

Coffee, or a Google Meet / WhatsApp call. We define the theme, dimensions, and palette of your piece.

02

Digital sketch

You receive an initial digital concept, then we refine the composition before painting begins.

03

Deposit

A 20% deposit after sketch approval secures your slot in the studio calendar.

04

Painting

Several weeks of work. Progress photos if you'd like to follow along.

No revisions after this stage begins
05

Varnish & delivery

After varnishing, the painting is shipped with insurance or handed over in person with its certificate.
