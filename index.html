From idea to your wall

  1. 1

    We talk

    Over coffee, Google Meet or WhatsApp. We settle the subject, the size and the colors.

  2. 2

    I sketch

    You get a digital sketch. We adjust it until the composition is right.

  3. 3

    You book a slot

    Once you approve the sketch, a 20% deposit holds your place in the calendar.

  4. 4

    I paint

    A few weeks at the easel. I can send progress photos along the way.

    No changes once painting starts.

  5. 5

    It comes home

    Varnished, then shipped insured or handed over in person, certificate included.
